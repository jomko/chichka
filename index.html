01 / AboutLviv / Ukraine

An evolving
practice.

Marichka Chichkova is a Ukrainian musician, performer and cultural producer whose work moves between traditional song, contemporary performance and independent cultural initiatives.

She performs, composes, arranges music and works with the voice — bringing folk traditions into dialogue with contemporary sound and theatre.

Alongside her artistic work, she creates places for music and people to meet: festivals, intimate concert spaces and cultural communities in Lviv.
